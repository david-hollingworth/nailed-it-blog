My last update was 10 days ago when I said I was just about to start the requirement definitions. Unfortunately, as soon as I began that exercise I realized the feature documents were just not ready. There were features in the wrong documents, a lot of "fluff" that didn't help towards the feature definitions as well as contradictions and ommissions.

I've now completed another full review of the features and I think things are in a much better place to move forwards. There hasn't been any major changes in the actual functionality, in fact the only thing I can recall is that all Habits are now parented from a Goal. Previously there was a case for standalone Habits, but the philosophy of Nailed-It is that it's for wworking towards your Goals; There are plenty of applications out there that allow you to manage just your habits, and Nailed-It is not designed to replace those. However, if you have a Goal, and a Habit can help you achieve that Goal, then Nailed-It has you covered.

This aternoon I shall start on the requirements again. Fingers crossed I get further than last time.
